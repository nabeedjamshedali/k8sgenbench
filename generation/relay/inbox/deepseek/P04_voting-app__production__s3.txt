Notes for production rollout:

- Replace `dockersamples/*:latest` images with your own registry-pinned, digest-referenced images. The provided compose files only reference upstream sample images; the Dockerfiles given are for building your own.
- The `postgres-secret` uses `stringData` for readability — in real environments source it from a sealed-secret/ExternalSecret/Vault and pin `POSTGRES_PASSWORD` to something strong.
- The `db` uses a `StatefulSet` with a single PVC; for HA Postgres, swap for an operator (e.g. CloudNativePG, Zalando). The app was written for a single Postgres instance, so keep replicas at 1 here.
- HPAs require `metrics-server`; PDBs and NetworkPolicies require CNI support (Calico/Cilium/etc.).
- The `seed` compose profile (one-shot DB seeder) is intentionally not included as a long-running workload; run it as a `Job` (`kubectl create job --from=cronjob/...` or a plain Job manifest) if you need to seed data.
- The `/healthchecks` scripts from the compose files are replaced with native probes (`redis-cli ping`, `pg_isready`, HTTP probes) so no hostPath mounts are needed.
- Ingress hosts `vote.example.com` / `result.example.com` are placeholders — update to your domains and TLS config.
